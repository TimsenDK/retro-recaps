# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Being patient with people who are new to this — someone recapping their first
  board is exactly who the project is for.
- Correcting data with evidence rather than authority.
- Accepting correction gracefully when your own entry turns out to be wrong.
- Focusing on what is best for the dataset and the people using it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Gatekeeping — treating inexperience as a failing.
- Encouraging anyone to work unsafely on mains-powered equipment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They may
remove or edit comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do
not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers
by opening a private security advisory, or by contacting the repository owner
directly through their GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
